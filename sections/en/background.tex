\subsection{Verification changes the MoE working set}

In one SD round, a draft proposes up to $\gamma$ tokens and the target verifies the
root and proposals together. Native target logits authorize the longest accepted
prefix and supply a replacement at the first mismatch
\cite{leviathan2023fast,chen2023speculativesampling}. Accepted length captures saved
target invocations, but not the expert state needed by that verification pass.

Let $E_{\ell,t}$ denote the experts routed at layer $\ell$ and position $t$. Batched
verification exposes
\begin{equation}
U_{\ell,\gamma}(t)=\bigcup_{i=0}^{\gamma}E_{\ell,t+i},\qquad
A_{\ell,\gamma}(t)=
\frac{|U_{\ell,\gamma}(t)|}
{(\gamma+1)^{-1}\sum_{i=0}^{\gamma}|E_{\ell,t+i}|}.
\label{eq:working-set}
\end{equation}
Here $A_{\ell,\gamma}$ measures cross-position overlap: the union is monotone in
$\gamma$, but shared routes slow its growth. If $C^{16}_{\ell}$ is the resident BF16
set, then $|U_{\ell,\gamma}\setminus C^{16}_{\ell}|$ lower-bounds the distinct
nonresident experts an ordinary verifier must supply even with perfect within-pass
reuse. Figure~\ref{fig:overview}(a) compares the supply paths for these missing
experts; the registered motivation trace quantifies the union and its misses.

Three pressures follow. Wider verification can demand more routed experts at once;
an independently parameterized draft stores another expert pool; and a second KV
cache duplicates sequence state. All three materialize closely related information
twice. This suggests the paper's organizing principle: reuse compact target state
across speculation and authoritative verification wherever exactness can be audited.

\subsection{Relationship to prior work}

Speculative and blockwise decoding establish target-validated generation
\cite{stern2018blockwise,leviathan2023fast}; self-speculative and quantized variants
reduce separate draft state or reuse representations across precisions
\cite{zhang2024draftverify,elhoushi2024layerskip,zhao2025qspec,wang2025subspec}.
MoE systems instead optimize placement, caching, prefetch, CPU execution, or routing
\cite{sheng2023flexgen,xue2024moeinfinity,du2024sida,kong2024swapmoe,hwang2024pregated},
while MoESD, SS-MoE, and SpecMoE specialize speculation for sparse models
\cite{huang2025moesd,zheng2026ssmoe,bang2026specmoe}. DFVG maps draft and verification
to complementary devices \cite{lu2026dfvg}; our narrower question is how one
memory-bounded GPU supplies a data-dependent expert union. The distinction is exact
supply: a requested nonresident BF16 routed expert is reconstructed bitwise from the
target-derived W4 object used to draft, with the target router and native verifier
unchanged.
